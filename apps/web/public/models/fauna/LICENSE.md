# Fauna asset credits and licenses

- **Goat** — [Poly by Google](https://poly.pizza/m/d7dImmjtF8E), licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Original mesh, materials and texture retained. AI Nation Builder added a 13-bone skin and original walk, breathing/idle and grazing animation. Attribution is retained here and in the in-game asset credits.
- **Shiba Inu** — [Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/m/y4wdQpg767), CC0 1.0. Original 46-bone rig and named animation clips. Creator's [Ultimate Animated Animals](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html) license statement also identifies this pack as CC0.
- **Chicken** — [Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/m/Z3RCoCYss4), CC0 1.0. Original rig and idle, peck and run animation clips.
- **Pigeon and Seagull** — original AI Nation Builder assets: anatomical tapered loft meshes, neck, head, beak, eyes, legs/toes, feathered wings and tail; eight-bone skeletons with original idle/walk/peck/fly clips. Generated by `scripts/build-fauna-assets.mjs`.

All files are served locally. No paid API, generation service or cloud account was used. `manifest.json` pins creator/source links, license, mesh modifications, SHA-256, byte size, bone count and clips. The build script downloads only the three explicit licensed public sources and generates the two original bird assets. It does not read credentials or call an AI service. Earlier candidate imports were rejected during visual review and are not distributed.
